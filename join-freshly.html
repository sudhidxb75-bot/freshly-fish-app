For Customers

Order fresh products online from Freshly

Freshly makes it easy for families to order fresh and essential products from one trusted platform. Customers can select their location, choose available products, and place orders through the Freshly website.

Based on location and service availability, Freshly connects the order to the suitable Freshly Hub for pickup or local delivery.

Customer Benefits

  • Multiple daily needs in one platform
  • Easy online ordering through Freshly website
  • Pickup from nearby Freshly Hub
  • Home delivery where available
  • Regular product updates
  • Freshly Reward Points
  • Referral rewards through Refer and Win

For Freshly Hub Partners

Start a local Freshly Hub in your area

Freshly Hub Partner is a local opportunity for people who want to serve customers in their own area. Freshly generates customer orders through the Freshly website, collects orders online, packs the products, and delivers packed orders to approved Freshly Hubs.

Hub Partners do not need to run behind customers or collect orders manually. Their main role is to support local pickup, coordinate home delivery where available, and provide reliable service in their assigned area.

Detailed earning structure, area approval, commission details, and operational terms will be shared only with shortlisted applicants after initial screening.

Hub Partner Benefits

  • Freshly generates orders through the website
  • No need to run behind customers
  • No need to collect orders manually
  • Freshly manages online order collection
  • Freshly packs and delivers approved orders to hubs
  • Serve customers in your own locality
  • Earn through hub handling and local delivery support
  • Monthly and yearly partner statements
  • Referral and performance-based benefits

Hub Partner Role

  • Receive packed orders from Freshly
  • Manage customer pickup from the hub
  • Coordinate local delivery where available
  • Support customers in the assigned area
  • Maintain timely and reliable service

Who Can Apply?

  • Shop owners
  • Small business owners
  • Home-based entrepreneurs
  • Delivery partners
  • Grocery, vegetable, and fruit sellers
  • Local area coordinators

Business Privacy

Freshly shares only the basic process publicly. Internal commission structure, sourcing, packing workflow, margins, area strategy, and backend details are shared only with approved applicants.
