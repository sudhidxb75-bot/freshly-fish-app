Choose how you want to join Freshly

Freshly connects customers, suppliers, hub partners, delivery partners and district partners through one local fresh commerce platform.

Become a Hub Partner

Benefits for Hub Partners

  • Start a local Freshly hub in your area with low operating complexity.
  • Freshly brings online orders, so you do not need to chase customers directly.
  • Earn through order handling, pickup support and local delivery coordination.
  • Use your existing shop, space or local network where suitable.
  • Increase local visibility and customer footfall through Freshly pickup orders.
  • Become part of a community-based fresh products and daily essentials network.

Become a Supplier

Benefits for Suppliers

  • Reach more customers through Freshly’s online platform and hub network.
  • Focus on quality and supply while Freshly supports order generation.
  • Get regular demand opportunities from customers, hubs and bulk orders.
  • List fish, meat, fruits, vegetables, groceries, food items and local products.
  • Expand your market reach without building your own delivery network.
  • Support a stronger local supply chain and community-based business model.

Become a Delivery Partner

Benefits for Delivery Partners

  • Earn by delivering Freshly orders in your local service area.
  • Simple delivery model: collect orders from hubs and deliver to customers.
  • No need to find customers yourself; orders come through Freshly and hubs.
  • Suitable for part-time or full-time local earning opportunities.
  • Work on nearby routes connected to Freshly hubs.
  • Grow with Freshly as more hubs and service areas are added.

Become a District Master

Benefits for District Masters

  • Lead Freshly’s expansion across your district or selected service areas.
  • Build and coordinate a network of suppliers, hubs and delivery partners.
  • Create income opportunities through district-level growth and operations.
  • Support local businesses, small suppliers, stores and delivery workers.
  • Develop bulk order opportunities from institutions, communities and businesses.
  • Grow step by step using Freshly’s community-driven hub model.

Start Freshly in My Area

Benefits for Customers and Local Communities

  • Request Freshly service in your area and help bring freshness closer to your neighbourhood.
  • Get access to fresh fish, meat, fruits, vegetables, groceries and daily essentials.
  • Choose hub pickup or home delivery where available.
  • Support local suppliers, hub partners and delivery partners through every order.
  • Help create local income opportunities in your area.
  • Make your area part of Freshly’s community-driven fresh commerce network.

Freshly

Freshly is a community-driven fresh commerce platform operating through district master hubs and local Freshly hubs.

All customer payments go directly to Freshly. Hubs and delivery partners do not handle cash.
